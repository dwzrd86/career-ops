# Jobbie alpha terms of use

**Effective date:** 2026-08-01
**Version:** 2026-08-01
**Applies to:** the invited Jobbie/Career-Ops web alpha in `web/`.

These terms describe an experimental product and are not a substitute for a
review by qualified counsel. They must be reviewed before a public launch.

## 1. The alpha service

Jobbie provides a verified-account workspace for manually recording job-pipeline
entries and changing their status. It is offered as an early alpha and may be
changed, paused, or withdrawn while it is being evaluated.

The service does **not** automatically submit job applications. It does not
connect to job boards, fill application forms, upload resumes, generate or
store tailored materials, or send messages to employers in this deployment.
You decide what information to enter and what actions to take outside the
service.

## 2. Accounts and acceptable use

- Register only with an email address you control, complete verification, and
  keep your password and verification links confidential.
- Use the service only for lawful job-search organization and in accordance
  with the terms that apply to any job site or employer link you open.
- Do not attempt to access another person's account or data, evade rate limits
  or bot protection, probe the service, interfere with its operation, or use it
  to spam employers or applicants.
- You are responsible for the accuracy, legality, and appropriateness of the
  information you enter. Do not enter credentials, government identifiers,
  payment-card information, or other information you do not want retained in
  this alpha.

## 3. Your data and privacy

The current data categories, storage locations, retention periods, export and
deletion process are in [[PRIVACY]]. Your use of the alpha is subject to that
notice. You keep responsibility for your job-search decisions and for deciding
whether to follow an external application URL.

## 4. Availability and changes

The alpha is provided on an experimental, as-available basis. Features may
fail, change, or be removed, and the service is not a backup system for your
career records. Keep copies of important information outside the alpha.

Material changes to these terms or the privacy notice will be published in the
repository release notes, reflected in the dated source document, and announced
in product before users save new career data after the change during the alpha.
Where a security or legal need requires an earlier change, notice may follow as
soon as reasonably practicable.

## 5. No professional or employment guarantee

Jobbie is an organizational tool. It does not guarantee interviews,
employment, compensation, suitability for a role, legal compliance, or the
availability or accuracy of any external job posting. It is not legal,
employment, financial, immigration, or other professional advice.

## 6. Suspension and termination

We may suspend or end access to protect users, investigate abuse, comply with
law, or retire the alpha. You may stop using the service at any time. See
[[PRIVACY]] for the current account-data export and deletion process.

## 7. Contact and security reports

For account-data or general privacy requests, use the contact method in
[[PRIVACY]]. For a vulnerability or suspected compromise, use the private
reporting channel in [[SECURITY_CONTACT]]; do not disclose a security issue in
a public issue or discussion.
